About FIA

FIA Fashion Intelligence is an enterprise software platform designed to help fashion businesses research emerging trends, explore fashion products and analyze market signals across available fashion information sources.

What FIA Does

The platform supports fashion trend discovery and analysis across apparel, footwear, accessories and retail categories. It organizes research evidence, source references and observations to help business users understand fashion developments.

Research Sources

FIA is designed to work with editorial publications, fashion brand and retail websites, and authorized social platform interfaces where access is available. Source availability depends on each provider's technical access, permissions and applicable terms.

AI-Assisted Analysis

FIA can use external AI services to analyze research text and, where enabled, customer-provided images. Each customer installation must have the appropriate permission and configuration before customer content is sent to an external AI provider. Local processing options may also be used where supported.

Business Use

FIA is intended for fashion brands, retailers, designers, market researchers and other businesses seeking structured fashion intelligence.
