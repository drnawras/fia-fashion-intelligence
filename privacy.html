Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nawras handles information in connection with FIA Fashion Intelligence (FIA), an enterprise fashion research and analysis platform.

1. Information FIA May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a customer, FIA may process search queries, fashion research results, source links and metadata, user-provided images and media, and application configuration and diagnostic information.

2. Customer Data and Installation

FIA is designed for dedicated installations. Each customer installation maintains its own application data and configuration. Customers are responsible for ensuring that content they provide or collect may lawfully be processed.

3. External AI Services

Some FIA features may send text or customer-provided images to external AI service providers for analysis. Such processing must be enabled in the customer installation and is subject to the customer's applicable permission and configuration. Customers can disable external AI processing where the relevant controls are available. FIA should not send customer content to an external AI provider without the required authorization.

4. Third-Party Sources and Platforms

FIA may connect to third-party websites and platforms through their official APIs or other authorized access methods. The information retrieved is subject to the relevant provider's terms, permissions and privacy policies. FIA does not claim access to private content or content unavailable through authorized means.

When a customer enables a platform integration, the integration may process the account credentials or access tokens required by that platform. Customers should protect these credentials and configure only the permissions necessary for their use case.

5. How Information Is Used

  • To perform fashion trend research and analysis requested by users.
  • To retrieve and organize authorized source information.
  • To maintain application functionality, security and reliability.
  • To troubleshoot technical issues and respond to support requests.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

Nawras does not sell customer data. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide enabled FIA features, including external AI providers selected or configured for the installation. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Data Security and Retention

FIA is designed to keep each customer's installation data separate. Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained by the operator of each installation. Data retention depends on the installation's configuration and applicable customer requirements. No software system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Customer Choices and Requests

Customers should use the controls available in their FIA installation to manage integrations and external AI processing. For questions about privacy, data handling or requests concerning information processed by Nawras, contact us using the address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as FIA features, integrations and applicable requirements evolve. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
